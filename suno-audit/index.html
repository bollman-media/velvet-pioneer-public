What Suno Can Do

Suno has evolved from a simple text-to-music tool into a full creative stack for audio. Here are the platform's most significant capabilities.

01 — Core
Text-to-Music Generation

The flagship — describe any song in plain language and Suno generates full tracks with vocals, instrumentation, and production in seconds. Supports simple prompts or the "Custom" mode for advanced control over lyrics, style tags, and structure.

All plans v5.5 model 10 songs/day free
02 — New in v5.5
Voices — Your Voice in Songs

The most-requested feature: capture your own voice and have it sing in any song you generate. Includes a verification system (biometric match of singing voice to a spoken phrase) to prevent misuse. Your voices are private — only you can use them.

Pro & Premier Biometric lock v5.5
03 — New in v5.5
Custom Models — Your AI, Your Sound

Upload your own original music catalog and fine-tune v5.5 on your unique style. Creates a personalized model that generates music sounding distinctly like you. Pro users get up to 3 custom models, making this a powerful tool for professional artists building an identifiable sonic brand.

Pro & Premier Up to 3 models Fine-tuning
04 — New in v5.5
My Taste — Personalized AI Curation

Suno learns your genre preferences, moods, and musical habits over time and shapes its recommendations accordingly. The more you create and engage, the more the platform adapts. Available for all users — the free-tier "hook" for personalization.

All plans Always-on learning
05 — Premier Only
Suno Studio — Generative DAW

The world's first "generative audio workstation" — a professional multi-track timeline editor with AI generation at its core. Generate unlimited stem variations (vocals, drums, synths), control BPM, edit pitch, export MIDI, and bring stems into any existing DAW. Studio 1.2 added Warp Markers, Remove FX, Alternates, and time signature support.

Premier only MIDI export 12 stems
06 — Pro+
Stems Splitting & Advanced Editing

Split any generated song into up to 12 individual vocal and instrument tracks. Add new vocals or instrumentals to existing songs without regenerating the whole track. Extend songs, remix sections, or inpaint specific segments. Upload up to 30 minutes of reference audio to guide style.

Analysis

Verdict

Suno is the clear market leader in consumer AI music, but its most aggressive bets — Voices, Custom Models, Studio — reveal where the product is headed and what the real moat looks like.

✦ Strengths
  • Best-in-class text-to-music quality at v5.5 — vocals are shockingly good
  • Voice cloning done with genuine consent architecture (biometric verification)
  • Suno Studio is a genuinely novel category — "generative DAW" didn't exist before
  • Personalization flywheel (My Taste + Custom Models) creates deep lock-in
  • WMG partnership signals mainstream music industry co-option, not conflict
  • $2.45B valuation with $250M Series C gives massive runway for model R&D
  • Free tier is generous — 10 songs/day is enough for casual viral growth
  • MIDI export bridges AI-native and traditional production workflows
✦ Weaknesses & Risks
  • Studio is Premier-only — raises paywall just as the most interesting features ship
  • Active RIAA litigation creates existential legal risk to the whole business model
  • No collaborative or multiplayer creation mode — sessions are single-user only
  • Custom Model training requirements are opaque — unclear data and track minimums
  • No native mobile app — web-only while Udio and competitors ship iOS/Android
  • Lyrics quality remains inconsistent — AI-written choruses often feel generic
  • "My Taste" radar is beautiful but offers no user control to manually tune preferences
  • Credits economy is confusing — 5 credits/song but pricing shown as credits/month
Strategic Opportunity

Suno is positioning AI music creation as a new instrument category — not software, not a service, but something you build a personal creative identity around.

The Voices + Custom Models + My Taste combo is a deliberate identity stack. Each layer makes your Suno feel more like "your sound" and less like "a generic AI." This is the right long-term bet — but it requires Pro/Premier which will limit viral growth to the free tier's simpler experience.

The WMG partnership is the real wildcard. If Suno successfully co-opts major label infrastructure — using their catalogs to train better models and offering artist-specific personalization — they could become infrastructure for the music industry rather than a disruptive threat to it.
