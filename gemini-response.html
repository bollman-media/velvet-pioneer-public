Help me prepare a one-pan meal Shashuka.

Shakshuka is a fantastic, one-pan meal that works just as well for a quick dinner as it does for brunch. Here is a simple, foolproof version that focuses on pantry staples.

Quick Skillet Shakshuka

○

Prep time: 10 minutes

○

Cook time: 20 minutes

○

Servings: 2–3

Ingredients

○

2 tbsp Extra-virgin olive oil

○

1 medium Yellow onion, diced

○

1 large Red bell pepper, diced

○

3–4 cloves Garlic, minced

○

1 can (28 oz) Crushed or whole peeled tomatoes

○

1 tsp Ground cumin

○

1 tsp Smoked paprika

○

¼ tsp Red pepper flakes

○

5–6 Large eggs

○

Fresh cilantro or parsley for garnish

Instructions

1

Sauté the aromatics: Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and bell pepper. Cook for about 5 minutes until the onions are translucent and the peppers have softened.

2

Add spices: Stir in the garlic, cumin, paprika, and chili flakes. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

3

Simmer the sauce: Pour in the tomatoes. Reduce heat to low and let the sauce simmer for 10 minutes until it thickens slightly. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

4

Poach the eggs: Use a large spoon to make small "wells" in the sauce. Gently crack an egg into each well.

5

Cover and cook: Cover the skillet with a lid. Cook for 5–8 minutes, or until the egg whites are set but the yolks are still runny.

6

Garnish: Top with fresh herbs and feta. Serve immediately directly from the pan.

Tips for Success

○

Don't overcook the eggs: They will continue to cook slightly from the residual heat of the sauce once you take the pan off the stove.

○

Texture: If you prefer a smoother sauce, use crushed tomatoes; if you like it chunky, go with diced

○

Add-ins: Feel free to stir in a handful of spinach or some sliced kale right before adding the eggs for an extra boost of greens.
